About

I'm a PhD student in Information Science at Peking University, working with Jun Wang and Qi Su at the Research Center for Digital Humanities. My research focuses on AI for humanities research: building language models and agents that help scholars work with primary sources, connect evidence, and develop interpretations.

More broadly, I'm interested in multimodal NLP, evidence-grounded reasoning, and cultural computing. Before joining PKU, I received my M.A. in Computational Linguistics and Language Technology from the University of Zurich, where I worked on explainable medical AI and LLM-assisted online discussions.
