About

I'm a PhD candidate in Information Science (Digital Humanities) at Peking University, affiliated with the Department of Information Management and the Research Center for Digital Humanities. I am supervised by Prof. Jun Wang and Prof. Qi Su. My research follows two complementary directions:

AI for Humanities. I develop AI methods that support humanities scholarship, including agentic workflows grounded in humanistic research practices. I also use multilingual and multimodal NLP to analyze large collections of cultural materials and uncover broad patterns of cultural evolution.

Humanities for AI. I draw on humanities perspectives to evaluate and improve AI's understanding of culture, values, and aesthetics, examining where large language models fall short and how these limitations can be addressed.

Before joining PKU, I earned an M.A. in Computational Linguistics and Language Technology, with a minor in Informatics, from the Department of Computational Linguistics at the University of Zurich. Under the supervision of Prof. Janna Hastings, I worked on using multimodal large language models to improve the interpretability of medical AI. I also hold a B.A. in Hindi from Beijing International Studies University, with exchange studies at Mahatma Gandhi International Hindi University in India.
